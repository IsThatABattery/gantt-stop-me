Realtime sync

Summary

Boards update live for every operator and astronaut, so nobody works from stale state.

Problem

Operators refresh to see changes and message each other before moving cards. Astronaut moves are invisible until reload, and two simultaneous moves can overwrite each other.

Goals

  • Card moves show up for everyone on the mission without a refresh.
  • Simultaneous moves never silently overwrite each other.
  • Astronaut activity is as visible as operator activity.

In scope

  • Connection handling and reconnects
  • Broadcasting card moves
  • Showing who else is viewing a card
  • Conflict handling when two people move one card

Out of scope

  • Editing while fully offline
  • Push notifications on mobile
  • Syncing across missions

Success criteria

  • A card move appears for all operators within [TARGET] seconds.
  • A conflicting move shows both positions and lets the second mover choose.
  • The Mission log records every conflict and how it was resolved.

Risks

  • Connection load on busy review days.
  • Conflict prompts could slow operators down.

Open questions

  • Should viewers outside the mission see live updates?
  • Who resolves a conflict when an astronaut and an operator collide?
